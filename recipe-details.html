Shakshuka

Eggs poached gently in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. A quick, filling dish that works for breakfast, lunch or dinner.

20 min · 2 servings · 6 ingredients

← Back to Recipes

Shakshuka was saved to your favorites. View My favorites

Ingredients

  • Eggs4 pieces
  • Ripe tomatoes, chopped500 g
  • Red bell pepper1 piece
  • Onion1 piece
  • Cumin1 tsp
  • Olive oil2 tbsp

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Stir in the cumin, then add the tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  4. Make four small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover the pan and cook for 5 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft.
  6. Serve hot, straight from the pan, with bread.

Tip: add a pinch of chili flakes if you like it spicy.
